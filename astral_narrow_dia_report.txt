DESIGN RATIONALE:
This design prioritizes practical constraints over theoretical DPPP optimization.
Target DPPP 1.5 would require 659 windows with 0.9 m/z width, violating:
- Minimum 2.0 m/z isolation window requirement
- Practical 300 window limit

The constrained design achieves:
- Narrow-DIA capability (~2-3 m/z windows)
- Realistic scan rates
- Practical implementation
- Good precursor sampling (DPPP 3.29)
